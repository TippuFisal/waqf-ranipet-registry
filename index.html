Tamil Nadu Waqf Board · Arcot, Walajah & Kalavai Taluks

Ranipet Waqf Land Registry

A searchable index of every Waqf institution and its recorded survey numbers for Ranipet District — transcribed from the Board's official furnished register so anyone can look up a Dargah, mosque or burial ground by name, village or survey number.

Source: Tamil Nadu Waqf Board, No.1 Jaffar Syrang Street, Vallal Seethakathi Nagar, Chennai–600001 — “Detail of Waqf Properties in the Arcot, Wallajah, Kalavai Taluk, Ranipet District,” Rc.No.786/2022/B7/VLR, dated 12.08.2022, furnished under the Waqf Act 1995 to the Sub-Registrars of Arcot, Walajah and Kalavai.

This registry was digitised from scanned pages of the original document for the purpose of search and reference. A small number of entries were partly obscured by the office seal in the scan and are marked flagged — please verify those against the original register before relying on them for any legal, registration or transactional purpose. Extent totals shown are approximate sums of the individually recorded survey numbers.

Note (as printed in the register): Apart from the properties listed above, any properties in a Sub-Registrar's jurisdiction which are Waqf by user/usage, which stand in the name of the above Waqf institutions, or which are registered as additional properties to the above Waqf institutions, or any properties mentioned in the revenue records as properties of the above Waqf institutions, shall also be treated as the Waqf properties of the above Waqf institutions.
