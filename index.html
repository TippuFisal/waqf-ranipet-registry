About the Ranipet Waqf Registry

This site is a free, searchable index of Waqf institutions — Dargahs, mosques, burial grounds and other endowed properties — recorded across the Arcot, Walajah and Kalavai taluks of Ranipet District, Tamil Nadu. It exists so that anyone researching Waqf land in Ranipet, whether a mutawalli, a land buyer, a researcher or a resident, can quickly look up an institution by name, village or survey number instead of paging through the original scanned register.

What is a Waqf?

A Waqf is property permanently dedicated by a Muslim for religious, pious or charitable purposes under Islamic law. In India, Waqf properties are administered under the Waqf Act 1995 through State Waqf Boards, such as the Tamil Nadu Waqf Board.

Which villages and taluks does this cover?

Every Waqf institution listed here falls within Ranipet District's Arcot, Walajah and Kalavai taluks. Use the village filter above the list to see institutions in a specific village.

How do I find a specific Dargah or mosque's survey number?

Type the institution's name, its village, or a survey number directly into the search bar at the top of the page. You can also narrow results with the village, survey-type and denomination filters.

Is this the official Tamil Nadu Waqf Board website?

No — this is an independent digitisation of the Board's official furnished register, built for public search and reference. Entries marked “verify” were partly obscured in the scanned original; check those against the official record before any legal, registration or transactional use.

Source: Tamil Nadu Waqf Board, No.1 Jaffar Syrang Street, Vallal Seethakathi Nagar, Chennai–600001 — “Detail of Waqf Properties in the Arcot, Wallajah, Kalavai Taluk, Ranipet District,” Rc.No.786/2022/B7/VLR, dated 12.08.2022, furnished under the Waqf Act 1995 to the Sub-Registrars of Arcot, Walajah and Kalavai.

This registry was digitised from scanned pages of the original document for the purpose of search and reference. A small number of entries were partly obscured by the office seal in the scan and are marked flagged — please verify those against the original register before relying on them for any legal, registration or transactional purpose. Extent totals shown are approximate sums of the individually recorded survey numbers.

Note (as printed in the register): Apart from the properties listed above, any properties in a Sub-Registrar's jurisdiction which are Waqf by user/usage, which stand in the name of the above Waqf institutions, or which are registered as additional properties to the above Waqf institutions, or any properties mentioned in the revenue records as properties of the above Waqf institutions, shall also be treated as the Waqf properties of the above Waqf institutions.
